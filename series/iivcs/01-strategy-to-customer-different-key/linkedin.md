# LinkedIn post: Everything between strategy and customer plays in a different key.

The companion post for [this article](article.md), as published.

---

Everything between strategy and customer plays in a different key.\
The people who write the score never hear it played.

I've spent close to thirty years watching organizations write good strategy and then never hear what became of it.

The failure is rarely the strategy itself. It's everything that happens between the executive offsite and the customer. Intent gets set in the language of objectives. Execution gets organized by department, because that's how the org chart works. Value gets measured at the end, in a survey, long after the decisions that determined it were already made.

And the one signal that would tell you whether the strategy was right — what the customer actually experienced — has no defined path back up to the people who set it.

This isn't a shortage of frameworks. Most large organizations run TOGAF, SAFe, OKRs, JTBD, and NPS at the same time. Each is a good instrument, well made and well played. None of them was scored to be played with the others.

The Insheights Integrated Value Creation System is our attempt to tune them. Three frameworks over one spine:\
OSM (Organization Strategy Model) governs the value streams.\
PTS (Product Transformation System) operationalizes them.\
CVDM (Customer Value Delivery Model) realizes value through them.\
The spine is value streams — the workflows your organization already uses to create and deliver value. That matters more than it sounds. It means IIVCS doesn't ask you to reorganize before you can start, and it doesn't replace the practices you're already running. It integrates them.

Two things I'd point to if you only have a minute.\
The feedback runs upstream. Customer signal becomes delivery learning, and delivery learning becomes strategic evolution — continuously, not at the next planning cycle.\
And it's measured. 3E for operational value, sequenced Use → Feel → Achieve. AER for behavioral signal. The 8 Values Model for what the organization actually generates. Most integrated frameworks stop before this part.

The whole system is published — frameworks, positioning, measurement models, practitioner guides, and comparisons against TOGAF, BIZBOK, ArchiMate, SAFe, Balanced Scorecard, and others.
